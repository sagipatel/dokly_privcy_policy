About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how [Your Company / Developer Name] (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the Doky mobile application (the “App”). By using Doky, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

What Doky does

Doky is a document utility app. Its features include:

  • Converting images and photos to PDF
  • Compressing PDF files to reduce their size
  • Splitting a PDF into separate files or pages
  • Merging multiple PDFs into one
  • Converting PDF pages to images
  • Other PDF and document tools that may be added over time
  • A Secure Vault for storing private documents on your device

Your documents and images

All document processing in Doky happens locally on your device. When you convert, compress, split, merge or otherwise edit a file, the work is done by the App on your phone.

  • We do not upload your images, PDFs or documents to our servers.
  • We do not operate any database or cloud storage that keeps copies of your documents.
  • We cannot view, access, read or recover your files.
  • The files you create are saved only where you choose to save them on your device.

If you choose to share a file from Doky using another app (for example email, WhatsApp, Google Drive), that file is handled under the privacy policy of the app or service you share it with.

Secure Vault

The Secure Vault lets you keep sensitive documents protected inside the App.

  • Encrypted storage: Files added to the Vault are encrypted using strong, industry-standard encryption (such as AES-256) and stored only on your device.
  • Locked access: The Vault is protected by a PIN, password, pattern and/or your device’s biometric lock (fingerprint or face unlock), depending on what you set up.
  • No cloud copy: Vault files are never sent to us and are not stored in any database we control.
  • Biometrics stay on your device: When you use fingerprint or face unlock, verification is done by your device’s operating system. Doky never receives or stores your biometric data.
Important: Because we don’t keep a copy of your files or your Vault password, we cannot recover Vault contents if you forget your password, uninstall the App, clear the App’s data, or lose or reset your device. Please keep backups of important documents.

Information we may collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, address or the content of your documents.

To keep the App working well, limited non-personal technical information may be collected automatically through the services listed in the next section, such as:

  • General usage statistics, such as which features are used most often
  • Advertising identifiers, if the App shows ads

This information is used only to fix bugs, improve performance and understand how the App is used.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Since we don’t store your documents, we have no documents to share. We may disclose limited technical information only if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the App.

Retention and deletion

Your files and Vault contents remain on your device for as long as you keep them. You are in full control:

  • Delete any file from within the App or from your device storage.
  • Clearing the App’s data or uninstalling Doky permanently deletes the Vault and all its contents.
  • Technical data held by third-party services is retained according to their own policies.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including on-device processing and encryption of Vault files. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and keeping both your device and the App up to date.

Children’s privacy

Doky is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
